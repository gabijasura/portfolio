Yennenga

01 / Client project

Monthly donor film

This animation was made for Yenenga Progress, a non-profit working with sustainable development in Burkina Faso. The goal was a warm, heartfelt film that makes people want to become monthly donors, by showing what steady support can do for education, food and the local community.

The story moves through hand-drawn landscapes, a heart, a small plant breaking through the soil and a school being built, before it ends on real photos of the kids and the message that a donation is an investment in a better and safer future.

I was the producer on this project. I planned the workflow and timeline, handled the communication with the client, gathered feedback and made sure the workload was shared fairly. Our team was very hands-on, so I also got to animate a few scenes myself.
