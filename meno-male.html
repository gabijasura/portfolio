01 / Internship at Bsmart

Catering hero film

During my internship at Bsmart, I led this project together with two other interns. I was responsible for the editing and animation, and I planned the film together with our producer intern. The goal was to promote Meno Male's catering service by showing off their Italian food and the warm, social feeling of their events.

The film shows everything from the wood-fired oven and the big wheels of cheese to people sharing pizza at a party. In the edit I kept the pace quick and the close-ups tight, so you can almost smell the food.

We delivered a 24-second hero film in 16:9 and 9:16, five stills with playful hand-drawn doodles that match the brand, and an animated GIF for their emails.
