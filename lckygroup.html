Lcky Group

Currently working at Lcky Group (formerly Glitnor) as a graphic and motion designer across four casino brands — Lucky Casino, Flax Casino, Happy Casino, and Vera&John. My work spans social media content, Meta ads, seasonal campaigns, display ads.

AI is a natural part of how I work. I build node-based generative workflows in Figma Weave — chaining image and video models to create stadiums, crowds and characters — and finish everything by hand in After Effects so it stays on-brand. I also use Claude as a production assistant, scripting After Effects comps and cloning Figma templates so one concept can become variations across brands and formats.
