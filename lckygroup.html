01 / AI Work

How I work with AI

I use AI every day, but the idea always comes first. It helps me create things I could never film, like full stadiums, crowds and 3D characters. It also takes care of the repetitive parts of production, so I have more time for the creative work.

  • Figma Weave
  • Claude
  • After Effects
  • Figma
  1. ConceptI start with the hook and the story. What should people feel in the first second, and what makes them watch until the end?
  2. GenerateI build node-based workflows in Figma Weave where image and video models are connected in one graph. That's how I make stadiums, crowds and 3D characters, and I can reuse the same graph for the next campaign.
  3. CraftThen I put it all together in After Effects. I composite, animate, add type and edit everything by hand, using the brand's own elements, the legal text and the safe zones for each platform.
  4. ScaleI use Claude as a production assistant. It helps me script After Effects comps, duplicate Figma templates for the monthly release ads for all four brands and automate thumbnail production, so one idea can quickly turn into many versions.
