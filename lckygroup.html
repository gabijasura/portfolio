Happy Casino ×
Pirots 4

A social ad for Happy Casino where the game's characters come to life. Instead of a static key visual, the characters were animated in Weavy — a node-based AI tool where image and video models are chained together into one workflow.

From there I took the AI-generated motion into my usual motion design process: timing, transitions, typography, the brand's graphic elements and the final 9:16 edit for Meta. AI does the heavy lifting on character animation, and I make sure it feels on-brand and tells a story in under ten seconds.
