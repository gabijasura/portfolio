01 / AI Work

How I work with AI

I use AI every day, but the idea always comes first. It helps me create things I could never film, like full stadiums, crowds and 3D characters. It also takes care of the repetitive parts of production, so I have more time for the creative work.

  • Figma Weave
  • Claude
  • After Effects
  • Figma
  1. ConceptI start with the hook and the story. What should people feel in the first second, and what makes them watch until the end?
  2. GenerateI build node-based workflows in Figma Weave where image and video models are connected in one graph. That's how I make stadiums, crowds and 3D characters, and I can reuse the same graph for the next campaign.
  3. CraftThen I put it all together in After Effects. I composite, animate, add type and edit everything by hand, using the brand's own elements, the legal text and the safe zones for each platform.
  4. ScaleI design the original, then Claude scales it. One agent per brand builds from my templates, and a separate reviewer agent checks every card against my originals before I see anything: no borrowed elements, no reused games, safe zones and legal in place.
02 / Creator footage

Streamer ads

Our brands sponsor Swedish streamers, and I turn their raw stream footage into Meta ads. I find the moment with the biggest reaction, put the game on top and the streamer below, and pull the captions from what they actually say, so the hook lands in the first second.

I cut several versions of each stream with different hooks and openings, so we can test which one works. In our esports campaign with Cartmens, the video I cut brought in more in deposits than it cost to run.

New streams come in every week, so I built an After Effects panel for it: I cut the clip once in 16:9, and the panel builds the 9:16 split and the brand endcard automatically.
