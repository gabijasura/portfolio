The Duck

01 / School project

My first walk cycle

The Duck is my first walk cycle, drawn frame by frame in Adobe Animate. It was inspired by Untitled Goose Game and, honestly, by my slightly out of hand collection of duck-themed things.

A walk cycle sounds simple until you try it. Getting the weight right in the body, the head bob and the little waddle took a lot of drawing, flipping back and forth and redrawing. It taught me more about timing than almost anything else I've done.

It's still one of my favorite pieces, and the duck lives on in the slot machine on my start page.
