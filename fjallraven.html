01 / Internship at Bsmart

Kånken Graphics

During my internship at Bsmart, I worked on an internal presentation for Fjällräven, introducing their Spring/Summer 2026 collection. My part was the Kånken Graphics backpack, a series with two animal-themed prints.

I animated the animals frame by frame so they could leave the backpack and move around the model. On one bag a little seal swims through the air, on the other a bird flies around and lands again. It turned plain product photos into small playful moments and gave the presentation some life.
