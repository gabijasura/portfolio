01 / About me

Motion designer in Stockholm

I'm Gabija, a Stockholm-based motion and graphic designer with a thing for glitchy, experimental visuals that are satisfying to stare at.

I studied media and design at Södertörn University, and later motion design at Hyper Island, which is where I really found my thing: making stuff move. After that I did my internship at Bsmart, working on projects like Fjällräven's Kånken animations and a catering film for Meno Male.

Today I'm a graphic and motion designer at Lcky Group, where I make social ads, campaigns and banners for four casino brands, each with its own personality. Lately a lot of that work involves AI. I build scenes and characters in Figma Weave and use Claude to take care of the repetitive parts of production, but the idea and the final touches are still mine.

I find inspiration in the most random everyday things, and I care a lot about the small details that make something fun to watch more than once.

When I'm not animating, I'm probably crocheting, painting, or making something with my hands. Creativity doesn't really have an off switch for me.
