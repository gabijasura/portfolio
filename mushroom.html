01 / School group project

Moving poster

A moving poster for Stockholm Mushroom Festival 2024, made as part of a group project in school. The brief was to design posters inspired by a real event. We came up with the concept together as a group, and then each of us made three posters of our own.

For this one I wanted to push myself, so I built a 3D mushroom in Blender for the first time. The caps stack up through the poster in soft peach and pink tones, with the type layered in front and behind them.

It was a lot of trial and error to get the texture and light right, but I love how it turned out.
