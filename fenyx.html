Explainer for Fenyx

Together with my team, I made a 30-second animated explainer for Fenyx, a company that trains people to work smarter in Microsoft Office.

The film follows a guy at his desk who is drowning in notifications on an old, 90s-looking computer. Once Outlook, OneNote and Teams start working together, the chaos turns into order, his desktop clears up and he can finally lean back. It ends on Fenyx's line "För att din tid är värdefull", because your time is valuable.

The goal was to make a pretty dry subject feel relatable and a little funny, using a simple flat style, clear colors and Swedish subtitles so it works with or without sound.
