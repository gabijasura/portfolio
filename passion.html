Passion

01 / Hyper Island

The dream of van life

For my final individual project at Hyper Island, the brief was to show passion through animation. So I started by asking myself what my passion actually is. I went through everything I love, like animation, crafting and design, until I landed on something that has been on my mind for a long time: the dream of van life.

The film starts with a girl on her sofa, daydreaming. Then we're on the road in a yellow van, driving through a desert with pink mountains and cacti, with a cat and a guitar inside. It ends back on the sofa, still dreaming.

I went for a warm, retro look with orange sunbursts and grainy textures, a bit like an old travel poster that has come to life.
