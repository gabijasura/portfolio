The Frog

01 / School project

Where it all started

My very first animation project, starring this little frog. I designed it in Illustrator, then brought it into After Effects and rigged it with the Duik Angela plugin so the legs could kick and bend naturally.

The frog swims across a pond seen from above, past a few lily pads. The water is made in After Effects too, with moving light patterns that keep the whole scene alive even though the frog itself is quite small in the frame.

Looking back, this is where it all started. Rigging something and seeing it move for the first time was what made me want to keep going.
