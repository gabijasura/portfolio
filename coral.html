Coral

01 / School project

Spotify Canvas

My first 3D animation. The brief was to make something abstract that fits one of three suggested songs and could work as a Spotify Canvas, the short looping visual that plays behind a track in the app.

The song I picked made me think of the underwater world, so I started building a shape that slowly grows and unfolds, almost like a coral reaching for light. Since a Canvas loops endlessly and sits behind the music, I kept the movement calm and soft rather than flashy.

Learning 3D from scratch was a lot, but it's also where I fell for the mix of shape, light and motion.
