Nightfall

01 / School group project

Game trailer

Jurassic Park: Nightfall is a cinematic trailer we made as a group, for a survival game concept set in a world full of dinosaurs.

Everything happens at night in heavy rain. A car drives through the jungle with its headlights on, two survivors sit by a campfire, someone lights a red flare, and then a dinosaur appears in the red light. We wanted the viewer to feel the danger slowly building before the title hits.

We combined a 3D environment with 2D characters that are more simplified and stylized, which gives the trailer its own look and makes the silhouettes really stand out against the dark.
