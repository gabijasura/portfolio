Pass the Ball

01 / Hyper Island

One ball, a whole class

Pass the Ball was a class project at Hyper Island. Everyone in the class animated their own short piece, and there were only two rules: a ball-like shape has to cross the screen, and the ball can be anything you want. All the pieces were then put together into one film.

In this part, the ball is a small orange planet floating through space. It opens up into an eye, the iris turns into a pink and white swirl that fills the whole frame, and then the eye closes and the planet drifts on. It's a short loop, so the challenge was making the transformation feel smooth enough to watch over and over.

I love projects like this. Seeing how differently everyone in the class read the same simple brief was half the fun.
